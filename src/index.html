Assumptions & sources

Silver is the second-lowest-cost benchmark; Bronze is the lowest-cost estimate. The subsidy line shows the available benchmark credit. The credit applied to Bronze cannot exceed its premium. Actual plans may have lower out-of-pocket limits.

These are KFF-style Marketplace estimates for the people marked “Enrolls.” Children eligible for Medicaid or CHIP generally cannot receive Marketplace subsidies. Uncheck their enrollment to model adult-only Marketplace premiums, while retaining the full tax-household size. State assistance and individual eligibility rules can change results.

When KFF’s rules indicate adult Medicaid eligibility, this page shows unsubsidized Marketplace costs and labels that status. Employer coverage also turns off the modeled tax credit and cost-sharing reductions. Tobacco selections flag a limitation: KFF does not estimate tobacco surcharges, so the premium curves remain unchanged.

Actuarial value describes an average population, not the percentage of your own bills an insurer will pay. Values and limits are pinned to 2026; this page does not estimate other coverage years.
